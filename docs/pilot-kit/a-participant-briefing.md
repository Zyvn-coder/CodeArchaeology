# Appendix A — participant briefing

**Hand this to the participant at the start of every session. Read the last two
bullets aloud, and take questions about the rules only — nothing about the
codebase, the questions, or the tools.**

---

You are being asked to answer six questions about a codebase you have not worked
on. There is no test of you here, and nothing is being recorded.

- **You have ten minutes per question.** A facilitator will tell you when the
  time is up. If you have not answered by then, write down what you have.
- **Every question has an honest answer, and "the history does not say" is one of
  them.** If you conclude the evidence is not in the repository, write that, and
  **you will be scored as correct when you are right**. Guessing is scored worse
  than saying you do not know.
- **Write down the evidence you relied on** — a commit, a file, a version, a
  test. An answer with evidence is scored better than the same answer without,
  and this is deliberate.
- **Write a confidence from 0 to 100**, honestly. It is compared against whether
  you were right, not used to judge you.
- **Do not use the internet.** The repository and the tools you were given are
  the whole world for the next hour.
- You may stop at any time, for any reason, and you do not have to say why.

---

*The six answer sheets are `c-answer-sheet.md`. There is nothing else for the
participant to read.*
